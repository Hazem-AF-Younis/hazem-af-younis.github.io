A required first-year course: engineering design in 2024 and 2025, then professional skills, ethics, and industry sectors in 2026. I have taught 525 students across three fall semesters, including two Fall 2025 lectures of 297 and 196 students.

* Meet weekly with the course faculty to review each session and help redesign the course; co-developed lecture decks, assignments, in-class activities, and video recordings used across all sections.
* Led industry sector weeks with student panels on semiconductors and on medical devices.
* Used before-and-after concept maps and weekly Connections assignments to build and measure engagement.
* Fall 2025 student ratings: "Overall, this instructor was excellent" averaged 4.60 (94% agreeing); "evaluated students fairly" averaged 4.66 (98% agreeing).
