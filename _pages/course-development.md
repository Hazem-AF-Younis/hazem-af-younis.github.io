Industry Sector Job Explorer, ECS 1100
------
*Week 12: Advanced Manufacturing, Biomanufacturing and Supply Chain · Fall 2026*

An interactive tool built for the industry sector weeks of ECS 1100, UT Dallas's required first-year engineering course. It collects 100 real job postings, ten for each of the Jonsson School's ten majors, split across internships and co-ops, early-career roles, and experienced roles. Students pick their major to see which skills employers ask for most often, then open any posting to read a short summary, its degree and eligibility requirements, and the full saved posting.

The goal is to make an abstract question concrete for first-year students: what does a job in this sector actually ask for, and what can I start building now? Postings were captured from LinkedIn on November 1, 2026.
