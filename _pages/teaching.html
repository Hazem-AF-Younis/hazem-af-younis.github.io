I teach the way I learned engineering: by building, measuring, and debugging. In large lectures I use worked examples, "what-if" prompts that ask students to predict how one change moves a whole system, and short peer activities. In the lab I teach one debugging routine that students and teaching assistants share: confirm power, probe stage by stage, compare what you expected with what you measured, and keep asking why until the root cause appears.

Courses I am prepared to teach

  • Digital logic and digital systems design
  • Advanced digital design with hardware description languages (Verilog, VHDL) and FPGA labs
  • Computer architecture
  • Microprocessors and embedded systems
  • Electric circuits and introductory ECE
  • Senior design
